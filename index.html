About

I'm an instructional technologist and faculty advisor with over a decade of experience at Salem Community College in South Jersey — the smallest community college in New Jersey, serving one of its poorest counties. That context shapes everything about how I approach this work.

My role sits at the intersection of Canvas LMS administration, LTI ecosystem management, accessibility compliance, AI literacy curriculum development, and direct faculty support. As a long-time adjunct professor in English and Humanities, I evaluate instructional technology from both an administrative and an in-the-classroom perspective — a vantage point that consistently shapes the tools and training materials I build.

I hold an MA in Writing Studies from Saint Joseph's University, with a linguistics and rhetoric background that informs my approach to pedagogy, documentation, and faculty communication. I am a CidiLabs ReadyGO Ambassador and GPTZero institutional partner, and I have been conducting longitudinal evaluation of generative AI tools in higher education since late 2022.

When the right tool doesn't exist, I build it. When a process is broken, I document it and fix it. When institutional needs outpace available resources, I find a way.
