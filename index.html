Instructional design

Selected course & curriculum work

A cross-section of design work — faculty development, course design, and institutional infrastructure.

Faculty Development

AI Literacy Training Course

A five-module Canvas professional development course built independently — Learn, Read, Practice, Teach — covering the current state of AI in higher education, curated readings, interactive tools, and assignment design frameworks. Built on personal initiative; currently being scaled for institution-wide use.

Faculty Development

Adjunct & Faculty Training Portal

A comprehensive Canvas-based resource hub covering LMS best practices, accessibility compliance, instructional technology tools, and course design standards. The primary faculty-facing documentation resource for the institution, maintained and updated continuously.

Course Design

HUM101: Introduction to the Humanities

Designed and taught a concept-first, thematic humanities course built entirely on OER materials — the first fully OER course at Salem Community College. Multi-disciplinary content across philosophy, literature, music, drama, and visual art, assessed through interpretive writing rather than content recall.

Course Design

ENG101: English Composition I

Teaches an experimental section integrating AI literacy as a core component of composition pedagogy — developing assignment models that support authentic student learning in AI-rich environments and building toward institutional adoption.

Accessibility

Institutional LMS Accessibility Overhaul

Overhauled the Canvas environment for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance — including theme redesign, system configuration corrections, and deployment of standardized accessible resource modules across all institutional course shells. Presented to the MSCHE site visit team in 2025.

Background

About

I've spent most of my career at Salem Community College — the smallest community college in New Jersey, in one of its poorest counties. That context shapes how I approach instructional technology: equity isn't an add-on, it's the starting point.

My role sits at the intersection of Canvas LMS administration, LTI ecosystem management, accessibility compliance, AI literacy curriculum development, and direct faculty support. As a long-term adjunct in English and Humanities, I evaluate instructional technology from both an administrative standpoint and an in-the-classroom perspective — which consistently shapes what I notice and what I build.

I tend to work on things before they're asked of me. The AI Literacy Training Course, the CLEAR tool, the Caption Wrangler — none of those started with a request. They started with a gap I noticed and a question of whether I could close it.

I hold an MA in Writing Studies from Saint Joseph's University. I am a CidiLabs ReadyGO Ambassador and GPTZero institutional partner, and I have been evaluating generative AI tools in higher education since late 2022.
